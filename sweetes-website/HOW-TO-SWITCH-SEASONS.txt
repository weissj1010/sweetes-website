SWEET E'S WEBSITE: HOW TO SWITCH SEASONS

1. Open the file called seasons.js
2. Near the top you'll see:   const ACTIVE_SEASON = "christmas";
3. Change the word in quotes to another season, like "everyday"
4. Save. The live site updates in a minute or two.

Or just ask Claude: "switch Sweet E's to everyday" (or any season).

Seasons ready now: christmas, everyday

THE MENU
The order page pulls products and prices straight from Erin's Square.
Change a price in Square and the website changes too.
- The season's Square category shows first (squareCategory in seasons.js).
- Items in "All The Time" show next (box sets, then treats).
- Hide an item from the website by adding its exact Square name to HIDE_ITEMS.

CHECKOUT
Orders go through Square and show up in Erin's Square app.
The website needs a Square key saved in Vercel as SQUARE_ACCESS_TOKEN.
