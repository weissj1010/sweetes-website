    My story

    Rooted in family tradition

    I'm Erin. For three and a half years I worked next to my Aunt Dena at Dena's Specialty Candy, learning every recipe by hand.

    When she retired in May 2025, she passed her secret recipes on to me. I opened Sweet E's that December, and I've been dipping, rolling and boxing them in small batches ever since.

    Real peanut butter, fresh nuts, rich caramel. Nothing mass-produced. Every piece is made by me, for you.

    Erin
